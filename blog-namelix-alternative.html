Namelix uses GPT-based models to generate short, brandable names. It works, but the experience is frustrating:

3. Looka — Name + Logo Combo

Looka generates names and pairs them with logo designs. The name generation is basic (keyword matching), but the logo editor is polished.

  • Free name generation
  • Logo design costs $20-65
  • No taglines or brand voice matching
  • Good visual preview of how names look as logos

Best for: Founders who want to see their name as a logo immediately.

4. Shopify Business Name Generator

Shopify's tool is straightforward — type keywords, get name suggestions with .com availability. It's free and fast, but the names tend to be generic.

  • Completely free, no signup
  • Integrates with Shopify store setup
  • No AI creativity — keyword-based only
  • No taglines, colors, or branding

Best for: Shopify store owners who want a quick, safe name.

5. Squadhelp — Human Creativity at a Price

Squadhelp runs naming contests where real humans submit ideas. The quality is high, but the pricing is steep:

  • Contests start at $199 (basic) to $999 (enterprise)
  • Takes 3-7 days for results
  • Includes taglines and domain validation
  • Overkill for MVPs and side projects

Best for: Funded startups that can afford $200+ and have time to wait.

6. Wix Business Name Generator

Wix offers a free name generator as a gateway to their website builder. Basic keyword matching with .com availability.

  • Free, no signup
  • Prompts you to build a Wix site immediately
  • Generic results, no AI creativity
  • No branding features

Best for: People who plan to build on Wix anyway.

7. BrandBucket — Premium Pre-Made Names

BrandBucket sells pre-vetted business names with matching logos and domains. Prices range from $1,000 to $75,000.

  • Curated, professional names with logos
  • Domain included in purchase
  • Extremely expensive — not for bootstrapped founders
  • Limited selection (you pick from what's available)

Best for: Enterprise companies with naming budgets.

5 Tips for Choosing a Business Name with AI

  1. Generate 30+ names before picking. The first 10 are usually obvious. The best names come from batches 3-4.
  2. Say it out loud. If you stumble, your customers will too. Test: can you say it in a noisy room?
  3. Check the domain before falling in love. Use NameForge's domain suggestions to verify .com availability immediately.
  4. Google it first. Search your top 3 names. If another company in your space has the same name, move on.
  5. Think about 5 years from now. "QuickBooks for Plumbers" limits you. "NameForge" doesn't.
